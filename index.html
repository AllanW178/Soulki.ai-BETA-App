What is Soulki.ai?



Have you ever wished you could talk to a character who truly listens—and talks back?

Well, now you can.

Soulki.ai is a website where you can roleplay, chat, and connect with AI-powered characters. Whether you want to create your own character from scratch or dive into a conversation with someone else's creation, Soulki.ai brings your imagination to life.

Characters That Feel Real
The magic of Soulki.ai is in the conversations. Every character has their own unique personality, voice, and memory. Some are witty, some are wise, some are a little chaotic. Talk to a legendary hero, a comforting friend, a fictional crush, or a completely original character you dreamed up yourself. They respond in real time, staying in character as the story unfolds.

Create Your Own Universe
Don't see the character you're looking for? Build them.

  • Give them a name, a personality, and a backstory.
  • Choose how they speak—formal, playful, mysterious, anything.
  • Watch them come to life as users around the world interact with your creation.


It's simple to start, but deep enough to build entire worlds.
